Trotting Wagons combat-wagon artwork
------------------------------------
The optional Scythed Combat Wagon compatibility resources are derived from the
Combat Wagon model, animation and color textures published in the Trotting Wagons
source repository at commit 402b48908f4048602c87a1f77282ee76a484cbd3.

Trotting Wagons is authored by Favouriteless and AbstractPortrait and is marked
All Rights Reserved. These resources remain attributable to their original authors.
They are loaded only as an optional compatibility restoration when Trotting Wagons
is installed; Recruits: RTS Camera does not replace or bundle that mod's runtime.
