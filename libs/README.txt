Bundled development dependencies for Recruits: RTS Camera 0.10.3-server.1.

- recruits.jar — required
- freecam.jar — required on client
- betterformations.jar — optional runtime integration, bundled for compilation

Siege Weapons is intentionally NOT bundled or compiled against. Artillery support goes through Villager Recruits' own optional compatibility layer and activates only when Siege Weapons is present and compatible.

Small Ships is intentionally NOT bundled or compiled against. Fleet support goes through Villager Recruits' Captain/SmallShips compatibility bridge and activates only when Small Ships is present and compatible.
