Current source version: 0.16.0-server.1
Minecraft/loader: Forge 1.20.1, Forge 47.2.0
Base dependency: Villager Recruits 1.15.2. Formation geometry, slot execution and tactical navigation are native to this mod. Freecam is bundled as a compile dependency. Order Relay, Serious Player Animations, Siege Weapons and Small Ships integrations must remain optional.

PROJECT GOAL
Turn Recruits combat into a Total War: Rome II-like RTS commander mode while preserving normal Minecraft outside RTS. Server authority and 100v100+ reliability matter more than flashy hacks. Use native Recruits mechanics where practical, but Commander may override broken attack/formation behavior.

0.10.8 HEADLINE CHANGES
- Fixed the user's confirmed 0.10.7 failure: routing hid the Commander card, but native Recruits retained the group and troops kept fighting. RoutedGroupManager now removes, saves and broadcasts the native group, clears all ordinary recruit AI goals and installs an exclusive escape goal. Native owner/team are retained during flight for diplomacy and campaign inspection; unit/team accounting is removed only on actual death or living escape.

Mounted combat/follow navigation receives a separate temporary 1.60x mount speed multiplier, excluded during formation pacing.
- Mounted melee charges now use measured forward run-up, speed and duration. Full strength requires 22 blocks at 0.72 blocks/tick; faster/longer sustained runs produce stronger impacts up to the configured cap. The first impact has up to 48 raw bonus damage before armor/shields, then 60% and 36% for the next two contacts. Actual swept mount contact can strike the front ranks without waiting for a slow melee cooldown.
- Charge momentum is finite: at most three contacts in a short impact window; stopping, sharp turns, flight and teleport jumps cannot generate charge power. Disengagement and a fresh run-up are required for another pass. Dismounted troops, ranged projectiles and ships do not receive cavalry impact damage.
- Cavalry inflicts extra morale pressure on foot troops while dealing close-combat damage: melee 2 points/second, ranged 8. Full charge shock is up to 14 versus melee or 30 versus ranged. Pressure is per defending group; shock uses the strongest-contact budget over two seconds, not a multiplier for every horse. Existing morale-item resistance applies.
- The ten [cavalry] server config settings expose all primary speed/damage/morale defaults. Existing configs receive defaults for new keys; do not tell users to delete their configs.
- Public RoutedDesertersEvent remains intact before actual living survivors are removed. See the campaign boundary below for batch semantics.

BATTLE ARCHITECTURE DIRECTION
Groups should increasingly behave as persistent tactical FORMATION objects. An ATTACK order is Group/Formation A -> Group/Formation B, not a single clicked entity. Individual soldiers get stable target assignments inside the enemy formation and reassign only when their target dies/becomes invalid. This same hostile formation target should eventually drive Volley, Skirmish, Charge and other group combat behaviors.

ROUTING / CAMPAIGN BOUNDARY
Commander owns tactical route/escape. It must not silently decide campaign consequences. On successful battlefield escape it posts RoutedDesertersEvent before removing tactical entities. Settlements or another campaign layer owns whether survivors become deserters, return to manpower, form bandits, etc.
The native group is removed at initial rout, before the escape hook. The hook still exposes ServerLevel, original group UUID, original owner UUID, living survivor UUID list and escape position. Process the supplied UUID list, not a lookup of the already-removed native group. Stragglers and previously unloaded members may escape in later batches under the same original group UUID. Each living survivor is emitted once during normal save/reload operation; campaign consumers should also deduplicate by survivor UUID. Dead recruits are casualties, not deserter-event survivors. Do not turn this into a silent campaign conversion.
